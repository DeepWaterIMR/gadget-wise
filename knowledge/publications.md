# Gadget (gadget2 / gadget3) bibliography digest

Scope: publications using or describing the Gadget fisheries modelling framework (Globally applicable Area Disaggregated General Ecosystem Toolbox). Compiled 2026-10-30 from (1) the local `publications` repo (`bibliography.bib`, gadget3 paper draft, miniCAPAM talk, size-platoons draft, 2018 ling paper, 2024 ICES ASC poster) and (2) web search plus API checks.

Conventions
- **G** = Gadget version: 2 = original C++ gadget (2004-), 3 = R/TMB re-implementation (`gadget3`), "2/3" = framework-level, "n/a" = not Gadget itself (context/method).
- **DOI status**: `[verified]` = DOI resolved in Crossref/DataCite during this session and the title matched. `[repo-bib]` = DOI copied from the local bibliography.bib and also verified. `[unverified]` = no DOI found/checked (URL only or none). No DOI has been invented.
- **Gadget-use status**: `[confirmed]` = abstract/source states Gadget. `[inferred]` = Gadget use inferred from a secondary source or from the gadget3 draft; check the full text before citing as a Gadget application.
- Entries are grouped by use category; some works fit several categories and appear once (cross-references in section 12).

## 1. Framework description, history and manuals

1. Begley J, Howell D (2004). An overview of Gadget, the Globally applicable Area-Disaggregated General Ecosystem Toolbox. ICES CM 2004/FF:13. DOI 10.17895/ices.pub.25349308 [verified]. G2. The canonical citation for what Gadget is (multi-species, multi-area, multi-fleet, likelihood-based, minimiser-driven). ICES notes conference abstracts should not be cited without reference to authors.
2. Begley J (2005). Gadget User Guide. Marine Research Institute Report 120 (Fjölrit 120), Reykjavik. URL https://www.hafogvatn.is/static/research/files/fjolrit-120.pdf [HTTP 200; no DOI]. G2. Mathematical description of every process and the input file format; cite for process equations (consumption, growth, maturation, migration, likelihood components).
3. Marine Research Institute (2005). Final Report: dst2 – Development of structurally detailed statistically testable models of marine populations, Technical Report vols 118 and 119 (Fjölrit 118/119), Reykjavik. URL via rafhladan.is (https://rafhladan.is/bitstream/handle/10802/32302/fjolrit-118.pdf and -119.pdf; server returned 403 to scripted access; no DOI) [unverified]. G2. Project report behind Gadget; vol 119 contains chapters on spatial structure, movement from tagging data, a Celtic Sea multispecies model, a North Sea herring model, and a simple Icelandic cod implementation (per table of contents seen in search snippets).
4. Stefansson G, Palsson OK (1997). Bormicon: a boreal migration and consumption model. Marine Research Institute Report, Reykjavik. No DOI [unverified; entry from repo bib STEFANSSON1997BORMICON]. Predecessor of Gadget (Gadget is described as a fork of Bormicon combining Fleksibest improvements, per gadget3 draft).
5. Stefansson G, Palsson OK (1998). Points of view: A framework for multispecies modelling of Arcto-boreal systems. Reviews in Fish Biology and Fisheries 8:101-104. DOI 10.1023/a:1008820702467 [verified]. G2 lineage (Bormicon). Conceptual rationale for area-disaggregated, length-structured multispecies models.
6. Guldbrandsen Frøysa K, Bogstad B, Skagen DW (2002). Fleksibest - an age-length structured fish stock assessment model. Fisheries Research 55:87-101. DOI 10.1016/s0165-7836(01)00307-1 [verified]. G2 lineage. Other predecessor whose ideas were merged into Gadget; cite for the age-length structured assessment concept.
7. Stefansson G, Palsson OK (1997). Statistical evaluation and modelling of the stomach contents of Icelandic cod (Gadus morhua). Canadian Journal of Fisheries and Aquatic Sciences 54:169-181. DOI 10.1139/f96-246 [verified]. Background (n/a): statistical treatment of stomach data that underpins the Bormicon/Gadget predation likelihood; Gadget use not stated.
8. Björnsson H (1998). Calculating capelin consumption by Icelandic cod using a spatially disaggregated simulation model. In: Fishery Stock Assessment Models (Alaska Sea Grant). DOI 10.4027/fsam.1998.38 [verified]. Pre-Gadget (Bormicon-era) spatially disaggregated model; historical context only, authorship initial from Crossref is "Björnsson".
9. Lentin J, Elvarsson BT, Butler W, Bartolino V (2025/2026 draft). gadget3: an evolution of the Gadget modelling framework. Draft manuscript (target journal Fisheries Research), local repo `2025-06-09-gadget3/index.qmd`; Quarto, authors Lentin, Elvarsson, Butler, Bartolino. G3. Not yet published as of the repo snapshot; see section 13 for a summary. Check publication status before citing.
10. Lentin J, Elvarsson BT, Butler W (2024 poster). Gadget3: An Evolution of the Gadget Modelling Framework. ICES Annual Science Conference poster (repo `2024-09-ices-asc-poster`). G3. Poster precursor of the gadget3 paper; same three-author list in poster header.
11. Elvarsson BT (2024 posting of earlier talk). RGadget: a R-package for development, testing and analysis of Gadget models. ICES conference contribution. DOI 10.17895/ices.pub.25682010 [verified]. G2. Describes the Rgadget R interface for Gadget2 (record posted to the ICES Library in 2024; original conference year not confirmed).

18. Vihtakari M, Elvarsson BT, Butler W, Lentin J (2025). gadgetplots: Plot gadget3 models. R package; https://github.com/gadget-framework/gadgetplots (HTTP 200). No DOI resolved [unverified]. G3. Standard diagnostic HTML report via `gadget_plots()`.

21. Ushey K, Wickham H (2025). renv: Project Environments. R package. DOI 10.32614/CRAN.package.renv [repo-bib, not separately checked]. Cited in the gadget3 draft for reproducible gadget3 versions.

## 3. Likelihood weighting, data weighting and uncertainty

22. Stefansson G (2003). Issues in multispecies models. Natural Resource Modeling 16:415-437. DOI 10.1111/j.1939-7445.2003.tb00121.x [verified]. G2. Source of the iterative inverse-variance reweighting heuristic for Gadget likelihood components; cite whenever explaining why components are weighted.
23. Taylor L, Begley J, Kupca V, Stefansson G (2007). A simple implementation of the statistical modelling framework Gadget for cod in Icelandic waters. African Journal of Marine Science 29:223-245. DOI 10.2989/ajms.2007.29.2.7.190 [verified]. G2. First full single-species Gadget implementation for Icelandic cod; also applies the iterative reweighting scheme. (The repo bib carries the unpublished 2004 version as taylor2004simple.)
24. Elvarsson BT, Taylor L, Trenkel VM, Kupca V, Stefansson G (2014). A bootstrap method for estimating bias and variance in statistical fisheries modelling frameworks using highly disparate datasets. African Journal of Marine Science 36:99-110. DOI 10.2989/1814232X.2014.897253 [verified]. G2. Spatio-temporally structured bootstrap for Gadget (Rgadget bootstrap); basis for uncertainty in ling and other assessments. An arXiv preprint (0807.3677) exists but was withdrawn in newer versions.
25. Francis RICC (2011). Data weighting in statistical fisheries stock assessment models. Canadian Journal of Fisheries and Aquatic Sciences 68:1124-1138. DOI 10.1139/f2011-025 [verified]. n/a. Alternative to iterative reweighting; cited in the gadget3 draft's future-work section.
26. Thorson JT, Monnahan CC, Hulson P-JF (2023). Data weighting: An iterative process linking surveys, data synthesis, and population models to evaluate mis-specification. Fisheries Research 266:106762. DOI 10.1016/j.fishres.2023.106762 [repo-bib, verified]. n/a. Random-effects based data weighting that gadget3 can now implement.
27. Maunder MN, Punt AE (2013). A review of integrated analysis in fisheries stock assessment. Fisheries Research 142:61-74. DOI 10.1016/j.fishres.2012.07.025 [repo-bib, verified]. n/a. Context for composite likelihoods in integrated models like Gadget.
28. Thygesen UH, Albertsen CM, Berg CW, Kristensen K, Nielsen A (2017). Validation of ecological state space models using the Laplace approximation. Environmental and Ecological Statistics 24:317-339. DOI 10.1007/s10651-017-0372-4 [repo-bib, verified]. n/a. One-step-ahead residuals; planned gadget3 diagnostic.
29. Trijoulet V, Albertsen CM, Kristensen K, Legault CM, Miller TJ, Nielsen A (2023). Model validation for compositional data in stock assessment models: calculating residuals with correct properties. Fisheries Research 257:106487. DOI 10.1016/j.fishres.2022.106487 [repo-bib, verified]. n/a. Residuals for length/age compositions relevant to catch-distribution likelihoods; the gadget3 repo also has multivariate Tweedie likelihood scripts.
30. Taylor IG, Doering KL, Johnson KF, Wetzel CR, Stewart IJ (2021). Beyond visualizing catch-at-age models: Lessons learned from the r4ss package about software to support stock assessments. Fisheries Research 239:105924. DOI 10.1016/j.fishres.2021.105924 [repo-bib, verified]. n/a. Rationale for the standardised gadgetutils/gadgetplots diagnostics.

## 4. Growth (beta-binomial length update), size structure and platoons

31. Stefansson G (2005). Growth models in population simulations. In: Final Report DST2 (technical report). No DOI [unverified; repo bib dst2growth]. G2/3. Origin of the beta-binomial length-group update used by Gadget; the gadget3 draft and miniCAPAM talk cite it for the growth transition matrix.
32. Elvarsson BT, Lentin J, Butler W (2025). Length based processes in gadget3. Abstract/talk for mini-CAPAM (repo `2025-12-11-miniCAPAM`). Unpublished presentation [unverified]. G3. Shows beta-binomial growth transition (parameters mean growth, beta, max length-group growth n), `g3a_grow_lengthvbsimple`, multspec growth, and an age-length model fitted without age data (blue ling); notes computation cost (about 50 length by 15 age groups, quarterly steps).
33. Elvarsson BT, Lentin J (2026 draft). Defining growth platoons in Gadget models (working title: a method to reduce computational complexity in age-length structured models). Draft, repo `2026-01-06-size-platoons/wd.qmd` [unpublished]. G3 (in development). Platoons defined by quantile cut points of K (and Linf|K) from a multivariate normal; follows the SS3 approach.
34. Rincón Hidalgo M, Gamaza M, Zúñiga MJ, Ramos F, Tornero J (2025). Decoding growth parameters of small pelagics: a critical examination of model effectiveness with a focus on the European anchovy. Frontiers in Marine Science 12. DOI 10.3389/fmars.2025.1467442 [verified]. G2 [confirmed]. Fixing mixed-effects VB growth parameters inside the Gulf of Cádiz anchovy Gadget model reduced run time and improved fit; good citation for externally estimating growth.
35. Gudmundsson G (1994). Time series analysis of catch-at-age observations. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series C 43:117-126. DOI 10.2307/2986116 [repo-bib]. n/a. Bespoke model with random deviations, cited as contrast to general frameworks.
36. Gudmundsson G (1995). Time series analysis of catch-at-length data. ICES Journal of Marine Science 52:781-795. DOI 10.1006/jmsc.1995.0075 [repo-bib, verified]. n/a. Catch-at-length analogue, relevant to length-based Gadget approaches.

## 13. Summary of gadget3 paper drafts in the publications repo

**A. `2025-06-09-gadget3/index.qmd`: "gadget3: An Evolution of the Gadget Modelling Framework"** (Lentin, Elvarsson, Butler, Bartolino; Elsevier preprint format for Fisheries Research; has submission tex and two rounds of responses to reviewers, so it is under review).
- Main claim: gadget3 is a complete re-implementation of gadget2 as an R package generating C++/TMB code, retaining the modular, process-oriented philosophy while enabling automatic differentiation, random effects, multivariate distributions and one-step-ahead residuals; already used in ICES advice for Icelandic slope beaked redfish, blue ling bli.27.5a14, Greenland halibut 5/6/12/14 and NEA Greenland halibut 1-2.
- Design rationale: (1) AD for convergence speed (the main gadget2 criticism; branching removed for tape compatibility); (2) flexibility: any number of natural-mortality processes, customisable process ordering, stocks as arbitrary homogeneous groups, fleets as stocks without length, stock structure independent of data resolution; (3) metaprogramming: model = list of actions (`g3a_*`, `g3l_*`) built from composable R formulas, `g3_formula()`, `g3_parameterized()`, with `g3_to_tmb()` or `g3_to_r()` outputs and a parameter template; (4) one-to-one mapping from gadget2 concepts, with `gadget2to3` and integration tests against gadget2; (5) software practice: CRAN releases, unit tests comparing R and TMB versions, GitHub Actions, renv for reproducibility.
- Data and likelihood: aggregation inferred from observation data frames (no aggregation files); `g3l_catchdistribution`, `g3l_abundancedistribution`, new `g3l_sparsesample_*` for individual-level observations; iterative reweighting retained (`g3_iterative()`) with a path toward Thorson-style random-effect weighting; diagnostics via gadgetutils/gadgetplots.
- MICE and projections: multispecies models by concatenating action lists, predation via `g3a_predate_catchability_predator()`, weight-jones growth with feeding level, `g3a_otherfood()` and `g3a_spmodel()` (surplus production stock) for low-resolution prey; stochastic projections via `g3_param_project()` for MSE.
- Worked examples: blue ling (age-length, 4 cm bins, combined IAGS/GHLE/SFE survey indices via Cochran method, commercial length distributions, quarterly landings, maturity) and a gadget2 vs gadget3 greater silver smelt comparison (under 1 h vs about 2.5 h; roughly 2 orders of magnitude fewer function evaluations).
- Future work: end-to-end multi-area plus tagging example, length-bin scaling and growth platoons, close-kin mark-recapture with migration, data weighting via random effects, one-step-ahead residuals.

**B. `2025-12-11-miniCAPAM` (talk "Length based processes in gadget3", Elvarsson, Lentin, Butler).** Explains the beta-binomial length-update of the growth matrix (mean growth, beta, max growth n, from Stefansson 2005), growth options (`g3a_grow_lengthvbsimple`, multspec growth), cost implications (about an order of magnitude more computation; typical 50 length by 15 age groups, quarterly steps; about 15 min optimisation for basic models), and fitting an age-length model without age data. Blue ling case: long-lived, deep-water, poor age data, fixed VB parameters from a related stock because free estimation destabilised the model.

**C. `2026-01-06-size-platoons/wd.qmd` (working title "Defining growth platoons in Gadget models", Elvarsson and Lentin).** Proposes replacing fixed age-length cells with a small number of growth platoons defined by discrete von Bertalanffy trajectories, following the SS3 approach; cut points taken from quantiles of a (multivariate) normal for K, or K and Linf conditional on K; claims large reductions in computational load while keeping size-selective effects. Draft with placeholders ("FIND CITATION"); results incomplete.

**D. `2018-11-01-Ling-paper` and `2024-09-ices-asc-poster`.** The ling manuscript is the Elvarsson et al. 2018 Fisheries Research paper (item 37): Gadget age-length assessment, MSE and reference points with a specialised bootstrap and MFDB-based reproducible data assembly. The poster is the gadget3 paper in poster form.
